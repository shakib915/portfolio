Research Experience

Undergraduate Thesis: Carbon-negative Mortar Using Biochar as Cement and Sand Replacement

2024 – 2025
Supervisor: Prof. Dr. G. M. Sadiqul Islam · Grade A+ · ACI VURS presentation ↗
  • Produced sawdust biochar at 400 °C in a low-technology pit kiln and used it to replace cement (1–5%) and sand (5–25%) by volume in mortar.
  • Characterized biochar and pastes by XRD, FTIR, Raman, TGA and SEM-EDS, with compressive strength, UPV and water absorption testing; 4% cement replacement raised compressive strength by 17.3%.
  • Built a cradle-to-gate LCA in OpenLCA (ecoinvent): 9.6% lower GWP at 4% cement replacement (22.9% per unit strength), and a net-negative GWP at 25% sand replacement.
BiocharXRD · FTIR · Raman · TGASEM-EDSOpenLCA

Banana Stem Fiber and Polypropylene Fiber Reinforced Concrete

2025 – 2026
Corresponding author · CUET & UCTC
  • Co-led the design and testing of alkali-treated banana stem fiber (BSF), polypropylene fiber (PPF) and hybrid concrete across three mix series: mechanical, rebound hammer, drop-weight impact and SEM.
  • The hybrid mix (50% BSF, 50% PPF) raised flexural strength by 63.4% and absorbed 3.79 times the impact energy of plain concrete.
  • In LCA, the hybrid mix cut the carbon cost per unit flexural capacity by 39%, the best flexural eco-efficiency of all mixes.
Natural fibersDrop-weight impactSEMCML-IA · ecoinvent

Microstructural Quantification of Ternary Blend Geopolymer Mortar

2025 – 2026
Co-author · Methodology, formal analysis, visualization
  • Co-developed an SEM-EDS image-analysis workflow (PCA and Gaussian mixture clustering) that separates N-A-S-H and C-A-S-H gels from unreacted precursors in fly ash–slag–metakaolin mortars.
  • Helped define a Material Efficiency Index (reacted binder fraction, 0.42–0.79), validated against selective dissolution, showing that precursor conversion cannot be inferred from strength alone.
  • Used the index to normalize cradle-to-gate GWP, which reordered the mixes' environmental ranking.
