Profile Summary

Hello! I'm Md. Shakib Khan, a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Bangladesh Military Academy (BMA). I completed my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) in 2025, ranking 15th in a class of 130. My research focuses on low-carbon construction materials, including biochar-modified mortar, natural fiber-reinforced concrete and geopolymer binders, with environmental performance evaluated through life-cycle assessment.

My undergraduate thesis explored sawdust biochar as a dual replacement for cement and sand in mortar. The work combined mechanical testing, material characterization (XRD, FTIR, Raman, TGA and SEM-EDS) and a cradle-to-gate life-cycle assessment in OpenLCA. I have since contributed to studies on hybrid banana stem–polypropylene fiber concrete and on quantifying the reacted binder in ternary geopolymer mortars through SEM-EDS image analysis. I am now building a machine learning model to predict the mechanical and durability properties of biochar-modified concrete.

My interests are moving toward combining laboratory testing with data-driven methods. I am seeking PhD opportunities where experimental and computational approaches work together to design sustainable construction materials.
